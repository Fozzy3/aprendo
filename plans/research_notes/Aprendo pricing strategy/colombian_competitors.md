# Colombian Saber 11 (pre-ICFES) competitive landscape and pricing, 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-11-05. USD shown at about 4,000 COP/USD. "Verified" means the price was read on the provider's own page (fetched). "Snippet" means it showed up in a search-engine summary of that page and I could not load the page to confirm it. "Derived" means my own arithmetic.

## Q1. Who are the main players?

### Takeaway
The market has four layers: (1) the big pre-ICFES/preuniversitario chains (Milton Ochoa, Helmer Pardo, Formarte, Instruimos, Deming, Multipruebas, SEP, Ipler, Geard). They sell B2C courses and also win large public contracts. (2) School-focused assessment and simulacro providers (iM-PROVE, Milton Ochoa's "Martes de Prueba", Xavia, Formarte simulacros, Hermanos Pardo). (3) A new group of AI-first web apps launched in 2025-2026 (IAsimulacros, Sabero, Lumina, SaberIA, Saber Más). Several of them already pitch school licences. (4) Free public options: the ICFES practice app and toolbox, Evaluar para Avanzar, Puntaje Nacional, and city and department programs (Medellín SaberEs, Bogotá SED, Palmira, Cundinamarca). The biggest single source of B2B money is municipal and departmental contracting through SECOP II.

### Inferences
- In public schools, Aprendo's real competitor is the municipality or Secretaría that buys a pre-ICFES package from a chain or university. Individual schools are not the buyer there.
- In private schools, the incumbent is iM-PROVE-style assessment plus simulacro bundles, and Milton Ochoa's Martes de Prueba and simulacros.
- The AI-first apps (Sabero, IAsimulacros, Lumina) already claim Aprendo's headline features: diagnostic, AI tutor, IRT score, school licences. "AI Socratic tutor" alone is not a differentiator.

### Gaps
- Instruimos, Formarte, Milton Ochoa and Helmer Pardo do not publish school (B2B) price lists.
- No public data found for Evaluamos, Tus Competencias, Santillana, SM, Norma, Sistema UNO/Compartir or Saberes Saber 11 platforms. Their prices are probably bundled into textbook or school-system contracts, but I could not confirm this.
- Pre-U and "Pre-ICFES Manuel Antonio" were not found.
- Platzi and Hotmart courses were not searched within budget.

## Q2. Prices, payment modality, inclusions, and B2C vs B2B

### Takeaway
B2C online courses list at COP 420k-1.43M "regular", but almost always sell at a 44-51% "discount" (≈ COP 350k-770k, USD 90-190) per cycle. Presencial courses run COP 690k-1.6M (USD 170-400). AI apps cost about COP 26k/month (USD 6.5). A standalone simulacro sells for about COP 15k-32k per student. Public buyers (SECOP) spend COP 50M-1.7B per contract, with no published per-student price.

## Q3. Typical price a family pays (presencial vs online) and per-student simulacro price for schools

### Takeaway
A family pays roughly COP 690k-1.6M (USD 170-400) for a presencial pre-ICFES course. A live-virtual course costs about COP 400k-800k and an asynchronous virtual course about COP 120k-450k. A school pays roughly COP 15k-32k per student per simulacro, or about COP 48k-96k for a 3-simulacro pack.

### Cited Findings
- Market guide (2026): intensive course (1-2 months) COP 300k-900k; semester course COP 150k-400k per month; virtual with simulacros COP 100k-250k per month; tutoring COP 35k-70k per hour; packages with material from COP 400k. — [Gestabiz 2026](https://gestabiz.com/co/blog/preicfes-preuniversitario-colombia-2026). This is a vendor blog with no named sources, so it has low reliability.
- A second blog puts best-value virtual pre-ICFES at COP 300k-600k. — [resultadosicfessaber11.com.co](https://resultadosicfessaber11.com.co/cuanto-cuesta-preicfes-virtual-colombia)
- Presencial data points: SEP COP 1.395M-1.615M; Deming presencial COP 690k (regular 1.3M); CursosPreicfes COP 850k. — see Q2 table
- Virtual data points: Milton Ochoa 350k-400k; Deming 420k / 730k; Multipruebas 430k / 770k; Formarte 1.36M; Geard 119k. — see Q2 table
- Per simulacro: Formarte 48k for 3 (2024); Xavia 96k for 3; Seamos Genios 15k for 1; Milton Ochoa 30k (2019). — see Q2

### Inferences
- A school-licensed Aprendo seat priced at about COP 30k-60k per student per year would cost the same as 1-2 standalone simulacros. It would cost less than a tenth of a B2C course. This is a strong anchor for B2B sales.
- B2C: an AI app at about COP 26k/month (IAsimulacros) sets the low-end ceiling for subscription pricing.

### Gaps
- No survey or official statistic on what share of students pay for pre-ICFES, or on average household spend.
- No data on what private schools charge parents when they bundle pre-ICFES into tuition.

## Q4. Differentiating features

### Takeaway
Reports by competencia, institutional dashboards and simulacros are table stakes in B2B. AI tutors and IRT scoring are now common among the 2025-2026 AI entrants. No provider was seen offering guaranteed scores. Where incumbents are thin: adaptive practice per subtopic tied to a teacher workflow (assignments, class plans) at a low per-seat price for public schools.

### Cited Findings
- Reports by competencia and micro-skill, comparisons by campus, grade and area, AI curation engine, adaptive clusters, printed books, AI-feedback tutor (iM-PROVE). — [iM-PROVE](https://www.improvecolombia.com/)
- Institutional report, improvement-opportunity report, curriculum adjustment recommendations, question-by-question feedback, paper or virtual delivery (Xavia). — [Xavia](https://xavia.com.co/servicios/simulacros-saber-11/)
- AI exam creation from PDF, cheating detection, metrics by student, classroom and institution for coordinators and rectors (IAsimulacros). — [IAsimulacros](https://www.iasimulacros.com/)
- Per-question AI tutor that does not reveal the answer, AI coach recommending what to practice, IRT score estimate, spaced repetition, AI question generation (Sabero). — [Sabero](https://sabero.co/es)
- Gamification and cash prizes: Instruimos "Olimpiadas del Saber", COP 54M in 2026 prizes. — [Instruimos](https://www.instruimos.com/)
- Physical printed guides mailed to students (Multipruebas) and live vocational guidance with psychometric tests (Multipruebas, Deming, IAsimulacros RIASEC). — [Multipruebas](https://multipruebas.co/preicfes-virtual-online/); [Deming](https://www.cursospreuniversitarios.com/preicfes-virtual-bogota/)
- Score guarantees: none found. Deming and Multipruebas pages mention no guarantee. Multipruebas states its courses are informal and give no certificate. Lumina explicitly states it cannot guarantee a score. — [Multipruebas](https://multipruebas.co/preicfes-virtual-online/); [Lumina](https://luminaeducation.app/preicfes-con-ia-colombia)
- Public programs bundle prep with vocational guidance and higher-education access (Medellín TOVO test, Palmira). — [Medellín](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/95-642-estudiantes-de-medellin-inician-los-simulacros-academicos-para-las-pruebas-saber-11/); [Palmira](https://palmira.gov.co/en-2025-alcalde-victor-ramos-reanuda-su-compromiso-con-preicfes-y-prueba-saber-11-gratuitas/)

### Inferences
- Aprendo's teacher panel (assignments, week plan, reports by competency) and school-admin seat licences overlap most with iM-PROVE (premium private schools) and IAsimulacros (low-cost AI).
- Vocational and career guidance is a common bundle item in both B2C and B2G. Aprendo's career-goal methodology (plans/meta-carrera.md) maps onto this buyer expectation.

### Gaps
- No feature-level detail was found for Martes de Prueba's current teacher reports, or for Helmer Pardo's and Instruimos' school portals.

## Q5. Market share, scale, funding

### Takeaway
There is no reliable market-share data. Scale claims are self-reported: iM-PROVE says 432+ private schools and 690k-1M students evaluated; IAsimulacros says 8,000+ registered students; Sabero says "hundreds" of active students. The addressable base is about 547k Saber 11 test-takers in 2026 across about 13k school sites. I found no funding rounds for any Colombian Saber 11 prep startup.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 Saber 11: 547,063 students, 13,028 sites (via Milton Ochoa ranking coverage). — search summary citing [Diario del Sur](https://www.diariodelsur.com.co/noticias/cambio-ranking-mejores-colegios-colombia-tras-pruebas-saber-11-top-10)
- iM-PROVE: 432+ private schools, 4 of the top 10 schools, 1M students evaluated in 4 years (690k+ in another section of the same page). Self-reported. — [iM-PROVE](https://www.improvecolombia.com/)
- IAsimulacros: 8,000+ registered students, 1,600+ simulacro questions, 800+ lessons. Self-reported. — [IAsimulacros](https://www.iasimulacros.com/)
- Sabero: 3,000+ questions, "hundreds of active students", "97% satisfaction" with no methodology given, 2025 pilot testimonials of +41/+56/+75 points. Self-reported. — [Sabero](https://sabero.co/es)
- Public scale: Medellín 95,642 students in simulacros (2025); Bogotá 11,792 students in 121 schools on the virtual track; Cundinamarca 16,717 test-takers (2026). — sources in Q1
- In SECOP II, Milton Ochoa (Asesorías Académicas Milton Ochoa y Bogotá SAS, plus Comercializadora Milton Ochoa SAS) is the only national pre-ICFES brand seen winning departmental-scale contracts in 2025-2026. PROASEDU repeatedly wins in Antioquia (Sabaneta, Guarne). Public universities (UIS, UTP, U. Distrital, UMAYOR, UdeA) are frequent contractors. — SECOP table in Q2

### Inferences
- The B2G market is fragmented across regional operators, public universities and foundations ("aunar esfuerzos" convenios). That favors a low-cost platform that local operators or universities can resell or bundle, in addition to direct sales.
- The AI-first B2C entrants are small (thousands of users), so no digital leader has emerged yet.

### Gaps
- No funding, revenue or market-share data found for Milton Ochoa, Instruimos, Formarte, Helmer Pardo or the AI startups.
- No Google Play download counts captured (not searched within budget).
- No press coverage (El Tiempo, Semana, La República) of pre-ICFES market size was found.
