# International pricing and monetization benchmarks for test prep and K-12 EdTech (2024-2026)

Currency note: the USD conversions use approximate rates for late 2025 and 2026, which I did not source and which should be re-checked before use: 1 USD ≈ 4,000 COP ≈ 5.4 BRL ≈ 88 INR ≈ 950 CLP. Local prices are always shown next to the conversion. "Yr" is the year of the source or page capture.

## 1. Test-prep pricing benchmarks (US, Brazil ENEM, Spanish-speaking LatAm, India)

### Takeaway
Test-prep prices fall into three bands. Free official practice (Khan Academy and Bluebook for the SAT) sets the floor. The middle band is cheap self-paced subscriptions or passes valid until exam day: about US$4-10 per month in Brazil and India, and about US$100-300 per year in the US. The top band is live courses and tutoring (US$700-1,900 per course, or about US$150-390 per hour, in the US). Brazilian ENEM platforms sell an annual pass paid in 12 installments of about R$33 (about US$6 per month) as the main offer, plus a short "intensivo" pass. PhysicsWallah built the largest exam-prep business in India on prices 10-20x lower than incumbents.

- For Aprendo, the closest analogue is Descomplica's ENEM pass at ~US$6 per month. Adjusted for Colombian purchasing power, that implies a B2C annual pass of roughly COP 150,000-300,000 per year, or COP 15,000-30,000 per month. This is my inference from the BR price and Spotify CO pricing (see section 5), not a sourced figure.
- In ENEM prep, the anchor for a premium tier is a human or AI-graded essay. ICFES Saber 11 has no essay, so Aprendo needs a different premium anchor: the AI tutor, simulacros with a full report, or a career-goal study plan.
- In Brazil, carrier bundles (B2B2C) gave cheap reach to low-income and prepaid users. Colombian carriers (Claro, Movistar, Tigo) are a possible channel.

## 2. K-12 B2B per-seat pricing

### Takeaway
Per-student site licences for single-subject adaptive practice in the US cost about US$5-12 per student per year, falling with volume. Multi-subject licences cost about US$14-25. Classroom engagement tools (Kahoot, Wayground) are priced per teacher, at about US$150-300 per teacher per year with small minimums. AI-tutor add-ons are priced much higher (list US$90 per student for Khanmigo for Districts). Brazilian learning systems (Arco, Geekie) price per enrolled student per year, but the figures are not public.

### Cited findings
- IXL Math site licence for July 2025 to June 2026, per student per year: US$9.81 (1,000+ students), US$8.72 (2,500+), US$7.63 (10,000+), US$6.54 (30,000+), US$5.45 (50,000+). Renewals rose 3% year on year. — [Henrico County co-op document](https://api.henrico.gov/coops/documents/612049578918407119/pdf)
- The South Carolina approved-software listing for IXL gives US$9.50-12.00 per student for one subject and US$14.25-18.00 for two. — [SC approved software](https://sites.google.com/view/sc-approved-software/ixl)
- Utah's state bid for IXL ELA and Math totals US$99.75 per student over five years, starting at US$18.75 per year. — [USBE pricing PDF](https://s3.amazonaws.com/midas-instructional-materials/3c7baf11-0d94-4c9f-b0d3-245dcfa088c6/IXLLearning_USBEPricing_ELA.pdf)
- IXL does not publish school pricing. The figures above come from procurement records. — [brighterly](https://brighterly.com/blog/ixl-cost)
- Kahoot EDU School & District (official page): Standard US$12.99 per teacher per month (US$155.88 per year), Pro US$17.99 per teacher per month (US$215.88 per year), minimum 3 licences. Another Kahoot page shows US$179.88 and US$299.88 per year. District pricing is by quote. — [Kahoot EDU](https://kahoot.com/schools/edu-school-and-district/)
- Wayground (formerly Quizizz): no official school price found. One reviewer reports "over $200 a year per teacher." — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/compare/156399-207437/Kahoot-vs-Quizizz)
- Khanmigo for Districts: one school-board order cites a standard Student Tutor add-on price of US$90 per student, with a first-year price of US$60 (a 33% discount). It cites a teaching-assistant list price of US$90 per teacher, discounted to US$72. Teacher access on its own is free. — [BoardOnTrack order](https://app2.boardontrack.com/org/X8qMBm/attachment/publicDownload/293326?s=X8qMBm), [khanmigo.ai/pricing](https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing)
- Arco calculates ACV bookings as enrolled students at each partner school multiplied by an average ticket per student per year. It runs 100% B2B, apart from the Me Salva purchase. — [BusinessWire, Arco FY2020](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210331005974/en/Arco-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2020-Financial-Results), [Brazil Journal](https://braziljournal.com/arco-educacao-compra-me-salva-para-entrar-no-b2c)
- Geekie is mainly B2B to private and public schools. Geekie One is a hybrid printed and digital system with AI-assisted grading of open-ended tests and a family app. One undated report cites 630 partner schools and 130k active students. — [Startupi](https://startupi.com.br/?p=252455)

### Inferences
- Pricing per student scales with enrolment and fits a school seat licence. Pricing per teacher fits teacher-led tools. Aprendo's current seat model matches the IXL and Arco pattern.
- Volume discounts in the US run from about 1.8x down to 1x across a 50x volume range (IXL US$9.81 to US$5.45). A tiered price list by seat count is the norm, and so is a small minimum (Kahoot: 3 licences).
- The US$90 list price for the Khanmigo add-on shows that vendors charge separately for the LLM tutor. Large first-year discounts (33%) are used to run pilots.

### Gaps
- No public per-student price for Geekie, Arco (SAE, Positivo, COC), Isaac, Savvas or Wayground.
- No sourced data on pilot structures (free semester, etc.) beyond Khanmigo's first-year discount.

## 3. AI-tutor monetization and LLM usage caps

### Takeaway
Consumer AI tutors cluster at US$4-12 per month. Khanmigo is the low end (US$4). Homework solvers (Gauth, Photomath) charge about US$10-12 per month or US$70-100 per year. Duolingo sells AI as a higher tier above Super (Max). It held Max back from low-monetizing markets such as India because of inference costs. Max reached 8% of subscribers by Q2 2025.

### Cited findings
- Khanmigo costs US$4 per month or US$44 per year for learners and for families. A parent account can add children. The pricing page lists no usage caps. — [khanmigo.ai/pricing](https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing)
- Duolingo Max made up 8% of subscribers in Q2 2025, up from 7% in Q1 and 5% a couple of quarters earlier. It is not offered in China, where launch awaits regulatory approval. — [iTiger Q2 2025 call summary](https://www.itiger.com/news/1176594056)
- Duolingo said it would not roll out Max in low-monetizing markets like India "due to current costs." — [Fintool Q1 2024 summary](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/DUOL/earnings/Q1%202024)
- Duolingo Max list prices conflict across secondary sources (US$14, US$19.99 or US$29.99 per month; US$167.88 per year). The official price was not verified. — [pingo.ai](https://pingo.ai/blog/how-much-is-duolingo-max)
- Gauth Plus: the US App Store shows US$11.99 per month. Other sources show US$21.99 per quarter and US$99.99 per year. — [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1542571008?l=en-us)
- Photomath Plus (Google): US$9.99 per month or US$69.99 per year. — [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/google-unveils-photomath-app)
- Chegg, a cautionary case for answer-based products: paid subscribers fell ~31% year on year to 3.2M in Q1 2025, revenue fell 30% to US$121M, and 22% of staff were laid off. Subscriptions had been 5.1M in Q1 2023, when management linked the slowdown to ChatGPT. — [getcoai](https://getcoai.com/news/edtech-takes-the-f-as-chegg-cuts-22-of-workforce-due-to-ai), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2023/05/02/chatgpt_chegg_ai/)

### Inferences
- Selling "answers" lost value once general LLMs became free (Chegg). An AI tutor monetizes better when it is tied to a structured curriculum, progress tracking and exam outcomes than when sold as a standalone Q&A tool.
- Duolingo's decision to keep Max out of India shows that LLM cost per user decides which markets get the AI tier. For Colombia, Aprendo should either cap tutor usage on cheap tiers or put the tutor in a higher tier.

### Gaps
- No primary source found on how Khanmigo, Duolingo Max, Gauth or Question.AI cap LLM usage (message limits, daily quotas). The Khanmigo page states none.
- No reliable Brainly Plus/Tutor or Question.AI price found.

## 4. Freemium conversion, retention, seasonality, exam-day passes, guarantees and family plans

### Takeaway
Typical freemium conversion in consumer apps is about 2-5% (median about 2.2%), and EdTech SaaS sits at the low end (about 2.6%). Hard paywalls convert much better (12% median) but reach fewer users. Duolingo is the top case at 9.0% of LTM MAU paying (Q2 2025). I found no hard data comparing a pass valid until exam day with a monthly subscription, or on score guarantees.

### Cited findings
- Duolingo Q2 2025: 10.9M paid subscribers (+37% year on year), 128.3M MAU (+24%), paid penetration 9.0% of LTM MAU (8.6% a year earlier). — [Duolingo Q2 2025 shareholder letter, SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)
- First Page Sage, based on 80+ SaaS clients from 2021 to 2025, puts average freemium-to-paid conversion at 3.7%, with EdTech lowest at 2.6%. This is a B2B and SaaS sample. — [summarized via userpilot](https://userpilot.com/blog/freemium-to-premium)
- Adapty reports median conversion of 2.18% for freemium apps and 12.11% for hard-paywall apps. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/markdown/freemium-to-premium-conversion-techniques.md)
- Lenny's Newsletter puts 3-5% as good and 6-8% as great for freemium self-serve products. — [Lenny's Newsletter](https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-a-good-free-to-paid-conversion)
- Descomplica's term-length options (12-month annual pass vs 6-month Intensivo) show how ENEM prep is sold around the exam calendar: long passes early in the year and short intensive passes near the exam. — [Descomplica help center](https://descomplica.com.br/vestibulares/central-de-ajuda/), [Descomplica Intensivo](https://descomplica.com.br/vestibulares/enem/gabi/)
- Family plans: Khanmigo's US$4 parent plan covers several children on one account. One source says up to 10 children under 18. — [khanmigo.ai/pricing](https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing), [aitoolsatlas](https://aitoolsatlas.ai/tools/khan-academy-khanmigo/pricing). Spotify Colombia prices Family at 1.65x Individual (COP 30,500 vs 18,500). — [Spotify CO](https://www.spotify.com/co-en/premium/)

### Inferences
- In exam prep, churn is built in: the user leaves after the exam. A pass "until exam day" (Descomplica annual, PW one-year batch, Magoosh 12 months) matches the value to the user's horizon and collects revenue up front. A monthly plan suffers drop-off after the exam and in school holidays. This is logical inference; no comparative data was found.
- A realistic B2C plan for Aprendo would assume 2-5% of active free users converting. Duolingo's 9% took years of product optimization.

### Gaps
- No sourced churn curves or seasonality for exam prep. Chegg filings and Duolingo letters would hold some but were not checked.
- No sourced evidence that score guarantees (Princeton Review or Kaplan "higher score guaranteed") raise conversion.
- No direct study comparing a pass until exam day with a monthly subscription.

## 5. Purchasing-power-adjusted pricing in LatAm

### Takeaway
Global subscription apps charge Colombia roughly 35-45% of the US price. Spotify Premium Individual is COP 18,500 (~US$4.60). The student plan is roughly half of Individual (COP 10,100), and Family about 1.65x. Brazilian ENEM prep sits at about US$6 per month. Platzi lists LatAm prices in USD.

### Cited findings
- Spotify Colombia (official page, early 2026): Individual COP 18,500 per month (~US$4.60), Duo COP 24,500, Family COP 30,500, Student COP 10,100 (~US$2.50). — [Spotify CO](https://www.spotify.com/co-en/premium/)
- Spotify raised Individual from COP 16,900 to COP 18,500 starting with September 2025 billing. Portafolio reports a Lite plan at COP 14,000, while an older Spotify promo page shows COP 12,900, so the sources conflict. — [Portafolio](https://www.portafolio.co/tecnologia/spotify-2025-la-plataforma-sube-sus-tarifas-y-esto-pagaran-los-usuarios-636971), [Spotify CO Lite](https://www.spotify.com/co-en/lite)

Argentina gets local-currency pricing (monthly from ARS 11,000, annual from ARS 89,000). — [Platzi precios](https://platzi.com/precios/?RewriteStatus=1), [Coderhouse](https://www.coderhouse.com/br/coderlibrary/platzi-sigue-siendo-una-buena-opcion-para-estudiar-online-en-2025)

### Inferences
- A sensible B2C ceiling for Aprendo in Colombia is at or below Spotify Individual (COP ~18,500 per month), with a pass until exam day as the main offer. Duo and family variants (Spotify's 1.3x and 1.65x) give a template for sibling or family plans.
- Paying in installments (Brazil's "12x") is a strong local convention for annual passes. In Colombia, annual prepaid with installments through cards or PSE would be the analogue.

### Gaps
- Duolingo Super/Max and Platzi COP prices were not found and need a direct check on the store or website from a Colombian IP.
- No data was found on how app-store regional pricing tiers (Apple or Google price tiers for COP) are set.

## 6. Lessons from failures and pivots (BYJU'S, Descomplica, Arco/Me Salva, Chegg)

### Takeaway
The failures share a pattern: B2C exam prep that grew on paid acquisition and high-pressure sales collapsed when demand normalized. BYJU'S fell from US$22B to near zero. Descomplica cut sales staff heavily in three rounds of layoffs. Arco, a B2B company, bought Me Salva to gain cheap B2C reach among public-school students. PhysicsWallah won with low prices and an organic YouTube audience.

### Cited findings
- BYJU'S grew on a sales machine that business press later blamed for its downfall: hard selling, financing-linked (EMI) course plans, impossible sales targets. Its expansion depended on costly acquisitions and debt. Demand dropped once schools reopened after COVID. Valuation fell from US$22B to roughly zero. — [Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/opinion/how-the-worlds-most-valuable-learning-app-became-a-trap-2844102), [Business Today, 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/from-22-billion-peak-to-debt-traps-and-lawsuits-how-byjus-empire-collapsed-533611-2026-05-28), [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2024/02/-lessons-byjus-failure-teach-startup-sector)
- Descomplica laid off 160 people in December 2023, 20% of the Descomplica UniAmérica group, after cutting 61 in June 2022 and 71 in August 2023 (~292 in total). Sales took the largest cut. The company had moved into higher education (it bought UniAmérica in 2021). It had raised a US$84M Series E in 2021 and was reported at breakeven in 2020 but still burning cash. — [Startups.com.br](https://startups.com.br/negocios/demissoes/descomplica-faz-nova-rodada-de-demissoes-e-dispensa-20-do-quadro/)
- Arco, then 100% B2B, bought Me Salva in March 2021 as its first B2C step. Me Salva had 900k+ users, 36% revenue CAGR over four years, and >70% of users from public schools, a segment Arco's private-school B2B model did not reach. — [Brazil Journal](https://braziljournal.com/arco-educacao-compra-me-salva-para-entrar-no-b2c)
- PhysicsWallah is described as doing "what Byju's couldn't": it went from a YouTube channel to a ₹3,480 crore IPO on low prices. — [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2025/11/06/from-a-you-tube-channel-to-rs-3480-crore-ipo-how-physics-wallah-did-what-byjus-could-not.html)
- Chegg lost ~31% of subscribers year on year by Q1 2025 as free LLMs replaced paid answers. — [getcoai](https://getcoai.com/news/edtech-takes-the-f-as-chegg-cuts-22-of-workforce-due-to-ai)

### Inferences
- In LatAm exam prep, pure B2C struggles to pay for itself when it relies on sales teams or paid acquisition. B2B school contracts (Arco, Geekie) give predictable annual revenue per student. Cheap B2C works when acquisition is organic (PW's YouTube, carrier bundles). This supports Aprendo's B2B-first model with a cheap or free B2C funnel.
