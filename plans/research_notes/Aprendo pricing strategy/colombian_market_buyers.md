# Colombian market for Saber 11 preparation: size, buyers, payments and tax (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-05. Every number carries its year. Prices in COP. Research budget was limited (~20 tool calls), so several questions are only partly answered; see each Gaps section.

## 1. How many students take Saber 11, and how are they split (sector, zone, socioeconomic level, schools)?

### Takeaway
About 490,000 students take Saber 11 each year as school students (ICFES count, 2025), or about 547,000 if calendar B, other sittings and adult cycles are included (Fundación EXE, 2025). Roughly 80% come from public (oficial) schools and 20% from private ones, and about 82% / 18% from urban / rural schools. They are spread across about 10,400 schools. The private segment, the one that can pay, is about 100,000-110,000 students a year.

### Cited Findings
- ICFES counts 489,880 students evaluated in Saber 11 in 2025, against 463,722 in 2014. The series is: 2014 463,722; 2015 459,028; 2016 474,527; 2017 469,835; 2018 467,490; 2019 467,413; 2020 457,214 (low point, COVID); 2021 486,276; 2022 488,130; 2023 507,387 (peak); 2024 494,458; 2025 489,880 — [ICFES, Nota de política "Doce años del examen Saber 11.º", Aug 2026](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NOTA-POLITICA-Doce-an%CC%83os-del-examen-Saber-11.o-4.pdf)
- About 82% of calendar A students attend urban schools and about 18% rural ones. About 80% attend public (oficial) schools and about 20% private (no oficial) ones. ICFES says the split "ha sido casi idéntica en los últimos cinco años y muy similar en los últimos doce años" — [ICFES policy note, 2026](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NOTA-POLITICA-Doce-an%CC%83os-del-examen-Saber-11.o-4.pdf)
- Fundación EXE counts 547,173 students evaluated in 2025. Its note says the figure "incluye todos los estudiantes que presentaron el examen en cada periodo (calendario A, B y otros)" and includes adult cycles. Evaluated students fell by 10,206 from 2024 to 2025, and 2025 was still about 11,000 below 2019 — [Fundación EXE, Análisis Saber 11 2025 (May 2026)](https://fundacionexe.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Analisis-Saber11_2025.pdf)
- Socioeconomic level (ICFES NSE 1-4; NSE 1 is the lowest, typically with no computer and no internet at home): in 2025, NSE 1 and 2 lost 15,217 students and NSE 3 and 4 gained 13,979. Evaluated students fell 2% in the public sector and 1.3% in the private sector. Rural evaluated students fell by 1,544 (-1.6%) and urban by 8,662 (-1.9%) — [Fundación EXE 2025](https://fundacionexe.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Analisis-Saber11_2025.pdf)
- Score gaps in 2025: public 253 vs private 289 global points, a 36-point gap (ICFES calendar A figure) — [ICFES policy note](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NOTA-POLITICA-Doce-an%CC%83os-del-examen-Saber-11.o-4.pdf). Fundación EXE reports a 31-point gap on its broader population — [Fundación EXE](https://fundacionexe.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Analisis-Saber11_2025.pdf). Rural private schools average 303.6 and rural public ones 230.1, a 73.5-point gap; in urban areas the figures are 279.6 vs 255.3 (2025) — [Fundación EXE](https://fundacionexe.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Analisis-Saber11_2025.pdf)
- For calendar A 2025 (exam on 10 Aug 2025), ICFES expected 640,000 people across Saber 11, Pre Saber and Validación, of whom about 596,000 were registered for Saber 11 calendar A — [ICFES](https://www.icfes.gov.co/640-mil-personas-presentaran-las-pruebas-saber-11-calendario-a/)
- The ICFES 2025 school ranking covers 490,533 students in 10,403 schools ("colegios"), with private schools averaging 41.6 points above public ones — [La República](https://www.larepublica.co/empresas/como-le-fue-a-su-colegio-en-las-pruebas-saber-consulte-aqui-4494531)
- Calendar B 2025: exam on 6 Apr 2025 (moved from 23 Mar), individual results on 6 Jun 2025 — [ICFES communiqué via Ámbito Jurídico, Mar 2025](https://www.ambitojuridico.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/COMUNICADO-Nuevafechapara-2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- Total market, one cohort per year: about 490,000 school students (ICFES 2025), rising to about 547,000 counting all sittings. Using the 80/20 split: about 390,000-440,000 public students and about 98,000-110,000 private students.
- Rural students number about 88,000-98,000 (18% of the two totals). Rural public schools have the worst connectivity (see section 7), which limits any web-only product there.
- About 10,400 schools have grade 11 students who take the test. If about 20% of students are private, private schools are likely more than 20% of schools, since they are smaller. This is not verified.
- The difference between 547,173 (all sittings) and 489,880 (ICFES school count) suggests calendar B, adult cycles and other sittings together add roughly 57,000 a year. Calendar B is mostly elite private schools. This is an inference, not an official number.
- The cohort is flat (about 460,000-510,000 for 12 years). Growth has to come from price and penetration, not from more students.

### Gaps
- No official 2025 count for calendar B alone. ICFES publishes it in its microdata (DataIcfes), which I did not query.
- No exact count of establecimientos or sedes offering grade 11 (DANE EDUC, SIMAT). The 10,403 schools in the ranking is the best proxy found.
- No distribution of evaluated students by estrato or NSE in absolute numbers. Only 2024-2025 changes were found. ICFES uses NSE, not estrato.

## 2. How do private schools pay for pre-ICFES and simulacros, and can they pass the cost to families?

### Takeaway
Private school fees are regulated by the MEN. For 2026, Resolución 019805 of 30 Sep 2025 caps tuition increases at 5.26%-9.1%, starting from the 5.10% CPI. Schools commonly pass simulacro or pre-ICFES costs to families as "otros cobros periódicos", and those must be approved by the local Secretaría. I could not verify the exact rules for these charges this round.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 increases: MEN Resolución 019805 of 30 Sep 2025. The base is CPI to Aug 2025 (5.10%), and increases range from 5.26% to a maximum of 9.1%. Inclusive-education schools may add 0.3%, and schools paying salaries at or above the Decreto 2277 de 1979 scale may add up to 2.2%. Schools fall under three regimes: regulado, vigilado or controlado. Each Secretaría de Educación authorises increases by administrative act before enrolment, based on the school's self-evaluation — [MEN press release](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/salaprensa/Comunicados/425951:Asi-sera-el-aumento-en-matriculas-y-pensiones-de-colegios-privados-para-2026); [El Espectador](https://www.elespectador.com/educacion/aumento-de-matriculas-de-colegios-privados-en-2026-cual-es-el-maximo-segun-mineducacion/); [Blu Radio](https://www.bluradio.com/sociedad/educacion/gobierno-fija-aumento-de-matriculas-y-pensiones-en-colegios-privados-para-2026-rg10)
- Commercial pre-ICFES providers set a lower price for students at partner schools: Grupo Milton Ochoa charges COP 350,000 for students of "colegios aliados" (2026-2027 course). This shows that providers sell to and through schools — [Grupo Milton Ochoa](https://preicfes.miltonochoa.com.co/)

### Inferences
- A private school can fund Aprendo three ways: (a) absorb it in tuition, which is capped at 5.26%-9.1% for 2026 and already committed to salaries; (b) a separate "otros cobros periódicos" line approved by the Secretaría, which is the usual route for simulacros (see Gaps); (c) parents paying a provider directly at a school-negotiated price, as with Milton Ochoa. Route (c) avoids the regulated fee schedule and is the easiest to start with.
- Because increases are set once a year (resolution in September, Secretaría approval before enrolment), a school that wants Aprendo as an approved "otro cobro" for the following year has to decide by about Sep-Nov.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the article text that regulates "otros cobros periódicos" (Decreto 1075 de 2015, section 2.3.2.2, and the annual resolution). Pending checks: whether they must be voluntary and itemised in the Manual de Convivencia, and whether simulacros may be compulsory. Read Resolución 019805/2025 in full.
- No data found on typical private-school budgets for evaluation materials or on the share of schools that buy simulacros.

## 3. How do public schools and Secretarías de Educación buy?

### Takeaway
There are two channels. (1) A school's own Fondo de Servicios Educativos (FSE): the rector can contract directly under a special regime below 20 SMMLV (COP 35,018,099 in 2026, Bogotá figure). (2) The Secretaría, district or department: public contracting through SECOP II, with large contracts (COP 600M-1,000M historical examples) for simulacros or preparation across thousands of students. Medellín's 2025 programme reaches 95,642 students with year-round simulacros.

### Cited Findings
- FSE contracts below 20 SMMLV follow the special regime of art. 13 Ley 715 de 2001 and art. 2.3.1.6.3.17 Decreto 1075 de 2015. At or above 20 SMMLV, the Estatuto General de Contratación Pública applies. The 2026 threshold in Bogotá: below COP 35,018,099 — [SED Bogotá Memorando 001-2026, tabla de montos FSE](https://www.educacionbogota.edu.co/intrased/sites/default/files/inline-files/MEMORANDO-No001-2026-TABLA-DE-MONTOS-FSE_.pdf)
- The rector is the ordenador del gasto of the FSE (without legal representation). Below 20 SMMLV, the rector follows the procedure adopted by the Consejo Directivo by acuerdo — [Acuerdo de contratación FSE example](https://lawinsider.com/es/contracts/armXgllVdRG); see also [Colombia Compra Eficiente concepto C-380 de 2026](https://relatoria.colombiacompra.gov.co/conceptos/c-380-de-2026/)
- Medellín (May 2025): 95,642 students in official and "cobertura" schools take academic simulacros through the SaberEs strategy. There are "universales" simulacros for grades 3, 5, 10 and 11 and focused ones for grades 10 and 11, run "durante todo el año". Medellín also invested COP 1,350 million to pay ICFES registration for 19,000 students. The source does not name the provider or the contract value — [Alcaldía de Medellín, 25 May 2025](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/95-642-estudiantes-de-medellin-inician-los-simulacros-academicos-para-las-pruebas-saber-11/)
- Valledupar (2015, old but the only contract with values found): contract 558 with the firm Helmer Pardo for "más de 900 millones de pesos" to prepare public-school students for Saber 11 (the article's "999.936.000" figure is inconsistent). The previous year's contract was COP 599 million with Edupol for 3,004 students. A council member criticised poor results and planned to refer the case to the Contraloría — [El Pilón, 23 Aug 2015](https://elpilon.com.co/general/denuncian-costosos-simulacros-para-pruebas-saber)
- Tolima (department) runs a free programme for about 10,000 grade-11 students with virtual classes and simulacros, using the "Saber IA" tool of the Evalúa platform. No contract value was found (search result summary; primary page not fetched) — search result, unverified primary
- Bogotá SED organises simulacros for public schools (3,000+ students in one round), with no contract value published in the article — [Bogotá.gov.co](https://bogota.gov.co/mi-ciudad/educacion/simulacro-prueba-saber-11-participacion-de-colegios-publicos-de-bogota)
- National entities also buy simulacros on SECOP. Example: Liceos del Ejército, 3 Saber-type simulacros plus a results report for grades 10-11 — [Colombialicita](https://colombialicita.com/licitacion/153638078)

### Inferences
- Per-student benchmarks: Edupol 2014 ≈ COP 199,400 per student (599M / 3,004, in-person preparation, 2014 pesos). Medellín's registration spend ≈ COP 71,000 per student (1,350M / 19,000), which is the ICFES fee, not preparation.
- A single school can buy Aprendo seats without a public tender if the annual contract stays under COP 35M (2026), for example about 150 students at COP 230,000 or 700 students at COP 50,000. That is the lowest-friction public channel.
- Secretaría contracts are larger but slower. They depend on the annual budget, which starts in January, and on electoral and political cycles. The Valledupar case shows these contracts draw scrutiny (Contraloría, council debates) when results do not improve, so measurable outcome reports are a selling point.
- Contratos interadministrativos (direct contracting between public entities, for example a public university running a Secretaría's pre-ICFES) are a common competitor channel in Colombia, but I found no 2025 example this round.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 SECOP II contract for a simulacro or pre-ICFES platform with both value and student count was found. Next step: query the SECOP II open-data API (datos.gov.co, dataset "SECOP II - Contratos Electrónicos") with terms like "simulacro", "pruebas saber", "preicfes", filtered 2024-2026.
- Purchase timing within the fiscal year (contracting months) is not confirmed. A plausible pattern is contracting in Feb-May so students are prepared before the August exam, but it is unverified.
- The mínima cuantía threshold for Secretarías in 2026 was not retrieved (it depends on each entity's budget).

## 4. B2C willingness to pay

### Takeaway
Family-paid pre-ICFES prices range from about COP 65,000 (online-only, PreICFES Fundamental) to about COP 1.8 million (in-person, Formarte Bogotá). Live-class virtual courses cluster at COP 350,000-900,000 (2026-2027 prices). Low-cost platforms also sell day, week and month passes (COP 3,000 / 10,000 / 30,000). Digital wallets are widespread (Nequi reported 28 million users), but card and household-spending figures were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Grupo Milton Ochoa, virtual PreICFES 2026-2027 (live Saturday classes on Teams, 3 ICFES-type simulacros): COP 350,000 paid upfront before 9 Nov, COP 400,000 after, or 3 installments of COP 150,000 (COP 450,000 total) — [Grupo Milton Ochoa](https://preicfes.miltonochoa.com.co/)
- PreICFES Fundamental: full 5-subject course COP 65,000, one subject COP 21,900, platform access COP 3,000/day, COP 10,000/week or COP 30,000/month (2026) — [PreICFES Fundamental](https://preicfesfundamental.com/)
- Grupo 500 Educación: course for calendar G 2026 / calendar A 2027 COP 700,000; year-long course COP 1,500,000 — [Grupo 500](https://grupo500educacion.com/)
- Preuniversitario de Colombia: virtual or in-person PreIcfes COP 900,000; 3-in-1 pre-university COP 1,950,000 — [Preuniversitario de Colombia](https://preuniversitariodecolombia.edu.co/cursos/)
- Formarte Bogotá Pre Saber 11: COP 1,795,000 list price, COP 1,615,500 paid in full, with 10%-33% early-payment discounts — [Formarte](https://formarte.edu.co/bogota/pre-saber-11/)
- Nequi reported reaching 28 million users at its 10-year mark (headline only; body not verified) — [Blu Radio](https://www.bluradio.com/economia/nequi-alcanza-los-28-millones-de-usuarios-a-10-anos-de-su-creacion-rg10)
- Bre-B, the Banco de la República instant-payment system, has operated since Oct 2025 and is reachable from bank and wallet apps (Nequi, Daviplata, Bancolombia, Davivienda, BBVA) — [Banco de la República documento técnico Bre-B, Feb 2026](https://d1b4gd4m8561gs.cloudfront.net/sites/default/files/publicaciones/archivos/documento-tecnico-bre-b-febrero-2026.pdf); [Noticias RCN](https://www.noticiasrcn.com/economia/bre-b-se-integrara-a-nequi-y-daviplata-para-el-2025-784579)
- Regulatory risk (Oct 2025): a Hacienda draft decree proposed a 1.5% withholding on purchases through Nequi, Daviplata and Bre-B — [Vanguardia, 24 Oct 2025](https://www.vanguardia.com/economia/2025/10/24/retencion-del-15-en-bre-b-y-nequi-frenaria-la-inclusion-financiera/)
- Household spending: the latest ENPH round is 2016-2017. Food is 31.2% of spending and housing and utilities 23.5%. Urban household spending is 2.2 times rural. The education share was not extracted — [DANE ENPH 2016-2017](https://www.dane.gov.co/files/investigaciones/boletines/enph/comunicado-enph-2017.pdf)

### Inferences
- Families who already buy prep (mostly private, NSE 3-4, urban) pay about COP 350,000-1,800,000 for a cycle with live teaching. A digital-only product with no live teacher competes with the COP 30,000/month to COP 65,000/course tier unless the AI tutor and simulacros are seen as a substitute for live classes.
- Installment plans (Milton Ochoa's 3 × 150,000) and early-payment discounts (Formarte, Milton Ochoa) are standard. Families expect them.
- NSE 1 students (no computer or internet at home) are effectively out of reach for paid B2C. Wallet payments (Nequi, Bre-B) lower the payment barrier for NSE 2-3 families without cards.

### Gaps
- Not verified: card penetration, the Banca de las Oportunidades 2025 inclusion report, Daviplata user count, app-subscription spending in Colombia, and price sensitivity by estrato. Next sources: Banca de las Oportunidades "Reporte de Inclusión Financiera 2024/2025", Superfinanciera, data.ai or Sensor Tower reports for Colombia.
- No survey data on what share of grade-11 students buy pre-ICFES.

## 5. Seasonality: exam dates and buying windows

### Takeaway
Calendar A (about 90% of students) sits the exam in early-to-mid August, with results in October. Calendar B sits in March-April, with results in June. Providers start selling the next cycle in October-November with early-enrolment discounts.

### Cited Findings
- Calendar A 2025: exam on 10 Aug 2025; individual results on 10 Oct 2025 — [ICFES](https://www.icfes.gov.co/640-mil-personas-presentaran-las-pruebas-saber-11-calendario-a/); [ICFES calendar A 2025 schedule](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2025_calendario11A-V02-28-abril.pdf)
- Calendar B 2025: exam on 6 Apr 2025 (rescheduled from 23 Mar); results on 6 Jun 2025 — [ICFES communiqué](https://www.ambitojuridico.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/COMUNICADO-Nuevafechapara-2025.pdf)
- Calendar B registration for 2025 opened in Dec 2024 — [El Universal](https://www.eluniversal.com.co/cartagena/2024/12/02/icfes-2025-abren-inscripcion-para-prueba-saber-11-calendario-b-y-validacion/)
- Milton Ochoa's early-enrolment deadline for the 2026-2027 course is 9 Nov — [Grupo Milton Ochoa](https://preicfes.miltonochoa.com.co/)
- Medellín's public simulacros begin in May and run "durante todo el año" — [Alcaldía de Medellín](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/95-642-estudiantes-de-medellin-inician-los-simulacros-academicos-para-las-pruebas-saber-11/)
- Private school tuition decisions: the MEN resolution comes out in September and Secretarías approve before enrolment — [MEN](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/salaprensa/Comunicados/425951:Asi-sera-el-aumento-en-matriculas-y-pensiones-de-colegios-privados-para-2026)

### Inferences
- B2C calendar A: buying peaks in Oct-Jan (early discounts for the next cohort) and again in Apr-Jul (last-minute). Usage drops right after the exam in August.
- B2B private: annual decision in Sep-Nov for the next school year (fee approval). B2G: budgets open in January, so contracting is plausible in Feb-May, before the August exam (unverified).
- Calendar B schools (mostly elite private) need the product live by about Oct-Mar.

### Gaps
- No 2026 exam dates retrieved (calendar A 2026 would have been in Aug 2026; check the ICFES cronograma).

## 6. Payment and tax practicalities for a SaaS selling in Colombia

### Takeaway
Card fees are about 2.6%-3.3% plus COP 300-900 per transaction plus IVA on the fee. Wompi charges about 1% for Nequi or QR, and PSE carries its own fee. A non-formal online prep subscription is most likely subject to 19% IVA: the art. 476 ET exclusions cover formal education by recognised establishments and virtual education "para el desarrollo de contenidos digitales", not general access to educational content. Electronic invoicing under DIAN Resolución 000165 of 2023 applies. Data on minors requires prior, express, informed authorisation from the legal representative.

### Cited Findings
- Gateway fees (2026 blog comparisons; they conflict with each other, so verify on official pages):
  - Wompi: 2.65% + COP 700 + IVA per card transaction (one source says 2.99% + COP 600); Nequi/QR about 1%; PSE 1.49% + COP 1,200 (one source)
  - ePayco: 2.64% + COP 690 + IVA (Davivienda accounts) or 3.29% + COP 700 + IVA (other banks); another source says 2.68% + COP 900
  - Bold: 2.89% + COP 900 online, or 2.80% + IVA + COP 500 domestic card, 3.50% international
  - Mercado Pago: 3.29% + COP 800 (paid out immediately), 2.99% + COP 800 (7 days), 2.79% + COP 800 (14 days)
  - PayU: estimates range from 2.79% + COP 300 to 3.29% + COP 300, negotiable
  — [btodigital 2026](https://btodigital.com/pasarelas-pago-colombia-comparativa-guia-negocio/); [mentoracolombia 2026](https://mentoracolombia.com/pasarelas-de-pago-colombia-2026-comisiones-wompi-bold-mercadopago/); [orbitlabco](https://orbitlabco.com/recursos/pasarelas-de-pago-colombia/); [bytechhub](https://bytechhub.com/blog/pasarelas-de-pago-en-colombia-comparativa-2026/)
- IVA: art. 476 ET num. 5 excludes services of establishments recognised by the Government (preschool to higher education and education for work). Num. 6 excludes virtual education only when it is for "desarrollo de contenidos digitales", as regulated by MinTIC (art. 2.2.16.3 Decreto 1078 de 2015). Other virtual education is taxed — [Gerencie](https://www.gerencie.com/servicios-de-educacion-excluidos-del-iva.html); [MinTIC](https://www.mintic.gov.co/portal/inicio/Sala-de-Prensa/Noticias/63377:Asi-es-la-reglamentacion-de-la-exclusion-de-IVA-en-software-y-servicios-de-educacion-virtual-para-el-desarrollo-de-contenidos-digitales)
- DIAN (Oficio 016687) treats selling web access to general training content and educational materials as a service taxed at the general rate — [Cr Consultores, DIAN Oficio 016687](https://crconsultorescolombia.com/servicios-excluidos-del-gravamen-dian-oficio-016687.php); see also [PM Abogados](https://www.pmabogados.co/tributario/dian-explica-que-actividades-de-educacion-virtual-se-encuentran-exentas-de-iva/?lang=en)
- Art. 476 num. 20 excludes software licences for the commercial development of digital content. MinTIC published a draft decree with 19 software categories and 16 thematic areas; its final status could not be confirmed — [MinTIC draft decree](https://www.mintic.gov.co/portal/inicio/Sala-de-prensa/Noticias/401734:Ministerio-TIC-publica-a-comentarios-Proyecto-de-Decreto-que-exonera-del-IVA-para-la-educacion-virtual-y-el-desarrollo-de-contenidos-digitales); [Ámbito Jurídico](https://www.ambitojuridico.com/noticias/tributario/tributario-y-contable/gobierno-propone-reglamentar-exclusion-de-iva-para-cursos)
- E-invoicing: DIAN Resolución 000165 of 1 Nov 2023 regulates electronic invoicing, equivalent documents and the "documento soporte", adopts technical annex v1.9, and was amended by Res. 008 and 119 of 2024 — [Resolución DIAN 0165/2023 text](https://normograma.mintic.gov.co/docs/pdf/resolucion_dian_0165_2023.pdf); [Sovos](https://sovos.com/es/cambios-regulatorios/iva/colombia-dian-emitio-la-resolucion-000165-para-el-sistema-de-facturacion/)
- Ley 1581 de 2012, art. 7: processing personal data of children and adolescents is prohibited except for public data and when it serves their best interest. Authorisation comes from the legal representative, considering the minor's right to be heard (Decreto 1074 de 2015). Educational entities must inform and train representatives about risks — [SIC concept via Ámbito Jurídico](https://ambitojuridico.com/sites/default/files/BancoMedios/Archivos/cpto-217036-20.pdf); [Asuntos Legales, Sep 2026](https://www.asuntoslegales.com.co/analisis/patricia-guzman-513211/el-derecho-fundamental-de-habeas-data-de-los-menores-de-edad-2518686)
- SIC Concepto 6089 de 2026 covers mechanisms to verify the legal representative's involvement in minors' accounts on platforms (secondary commentary) — [GyD Consulting](https://www.gydconsulting.com/medidas-datos-menores-de-edad-plataformas-sic-6089/)
- A SIC authorisation model for minors in academic events requires signatures from both the institution's legal representative and the minor's legal representative — [SIC form CS02-F17](https://sigi.sic.gov.co/SIGI/files/mod_documentos/documentos/CS02-F17/versiones//CS02-F17_V2.docx)

### Inferences
- Unless Aprendo is offered by a recognised educational establishment, or qualifies under the digital-content exclusion (unlikely for exam prep), assume 19% IVA on subscriptions. A COP 30,000 B2C price then nets about COP 25,210 before gateway fees. Public-sector buyers will expect IVA-inclusive quotes. Confirm with a tax adviser.
- Effective gateway cost on a small COP 30,000 ticket: about 2.65% + 700 ≈ COP 1,500 (≈5%) on card, before IVA on the fee. Fixed per-transaction fees penalise low monthly prices, which favours annual or per-cycle B2C plans and Nequi/QR (≈1%).
- Data protection: in B2B the school collects parental authorisation and Aprendo acts as data processor (encargado), which is simpler. In B2C Aprendo becomes data controller (responsable) for minors and must verify parental authorisation, which adds onboarding friction. Most grade-11 students are 15-17.

### Gaps
- Official gateway pricing pages were not fetched. The figures come from 2026 blogs that disagree with each other.
- Whether Aprendo must register as responsable de IVA depends on revenue thresholds and legal form, which were not researched.
- The final status of the MinTIC decree on art. 476 num. 6 and 20 is unclear.

## 7. Signals of EdTech adoption in Colombian schools

### Takeaway
Connectivity is the binding constraint in public and rural schools. In the most recent ICFES/DANE EDUC table, 63.8% of public sedes and 57.2% of rural sedes had internet, against 98.8% of private sedes. Computing equipment is more widespread than connectivity.

### Cited Findings
- Share of sedes with internet (ICFES table from DANE EDUC; the text says the years are 2015 and 2024): public 63.8%, private 98.8%, urban 95.0%, rural 57.2%. In the other year shown: public 38.0%, private 94.2%, urban 89.5%, rural 28.0%. In that same year, computing equipment was present in 91.6% of public and 97.5% of private sedes. "Bienes TIC" in the 2024 panel: public 77.0%, private 94.5%, rural 73.6% — [ICFES policy note, Aug 2026 (Tabla 1)](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NOTA-POLITICA-Doce-an%CC%83os-del-examen-Saber-11.o-4.pdf). Caveat: the table's panel headers read "2024" and "2025" while the text says "2015 y 2024". The lower-internet panel is most likely 2015.
- Computadores para Educar: Decreto 1326 de 2023 sends 70% of its yearly equipment to sedes from a general pool. Historical totals are 300,500+ computers delivered to about 24,000 sedes (undated). For 2025 the programme was defining allocation criteria (internet availability, rural, PDET municipalities) and planned AI and robotics programmes — [Decreto 1326 de 2023](https://normograma.mintic.gov.co/docs/pdf/decreto_1326_2023.pdf); [DPL News 2025](https://dplnews.com/computadores-para-educar-se-fortalecera-2025-colombia/); [MinTIC](https://mintic.gov.co/portal/inicio/Sala-de-prensa/Noticias/125574:Computadores-para-Educar-entregara-77-000-equipos-a-estudiantes-y-docentes-de-todo-el-pais)
- Secretarías already deploy digital tools for Saber 11 prep: Tolima uses "Saber IA" from the Evalúa platform with about 10,000 grade-11 students (search summary, primary not fetched). Medellín runs year-round simulacros for 95,642 students — [Medellín](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/95-642-estudiantes-de-medellin-inician-los-simulacros-academicos-para-las-pruebas-saber-11/)

### Inferences
- A web product sells easily to private and urban public schools (≈95%+ internet). For rural public schools (≈57% internet), offline-tolerant or low-bandwidth modes, or printable simulacros, would be needed to win Secretaría-wide contracts.
- An AI-based competitor (Evalúa "Saber IA") already holds at least one departmental deployment. The B2G market is not empty.

### Gaps
- No 2025 data on devices per student or on connectivity speed. Next source: DANE EDUC 2024 microdata and MinTIC "Conexión Total" reports.

## Cross-cutting market sizing (inference, arithmetic only)

- Cohort ≈ 490,000 (ICFES 2025 school count) to 547,000 (all sittings). Private ≈ 100,000. Public ≈ 390,000-440,000.
- Illustrative annual revenue at full penetration (not forecasts):
  - Private schools at COP 50,000/student/year: 100,000 × 50,000 ≈ COP 5,000M (~USD 1.2M at about COP 4,000/USD; exchange rate not sourced).
  - Public students via Secretarías at COP 30,000/student: 400,000 × 30,000 ≈ COP 12,000M.
  - B2C at COP 65,000-350,000 per cycle for, say, 10% of the cohort (≈49,000 students): ≈ COP 3,200M-17,000M.
- Historical public spend benchmarks: about COP 200,000/student for in-person prep (Edupol 2014) and contracts of COP 600M-1,000M per municipality (Valledupar 2014-2015). A per-seat digital price far below these leaves room for a Secretaría to cover many more students for the same budget.
