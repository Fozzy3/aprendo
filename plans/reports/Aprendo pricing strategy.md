# Sell seats to private schools first

Aprendo should run a hybrid model, and the order matters. It should **lead with per-student seat licences sold to private schools for each grade-11 cohort**, run a **low-priced pass "until results day" for families** as a cheap organic funnel, and **reach the public sector first through single-school purchases under the COP 35M direct-contracting threshold and through partners that already win municipal tenders**. Direct bids to Secretarías should wait until 2028. The reasoning starts with the market. The Saber 11 cohort has been flat at about **460,000-510,000 students for twelve years**. Only about **20% (≈100,000) study in private schools**, and those schools have **98.8% internet coverage**, against 57% in rural schools. Public money is larger (single municipal contracts reach **COP 50M-1.74B**) but slow, political and held by incumbents. B2C is crowded with AI apps at about **COP 26,000 a month** and with free government programmes. Aprendo's headline features (diagnostic, AI tutor, IRT score, school licences) are already claimed by at least five 2025-2026 AI entrants. The open gap is **adaptive practice tied to a teacher workflow at a per-seat price below one standalone simulacro pack**. The recommended list price is **COP 80,000 per grade-11 student per cycle before IVA**, with a COP 45,000 simulacros-only tier and a B2C pass at **COP 139,000-169,000 with IVA included**. LLM cost is not the constraint: a heavy tutor user costs about USD 1 a year. The constraint is legal. ICFES forbids any direct or indirect commercial use of its items, and Aprendo's current bank is extracted from ICFES booklets. No paid tier, and arguably no free funnel, can launch until an original item bank replaces it.

## A flat 490,000-student cohort splits into three buyers with different money

ICFES counted **489,880 students in Saber 11 in 2025**, against 463,722 in 2014. The cohort peaked at 507,387 in 2023 and has not grown ([ICFES policy note, Aug 2026](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NOTA-POLITICA-Doce-an%CC%83os-del-examen-Saber-11.o-4.pdf)). Counting calendar B, other sittings and adult cycles, Fundación EXE reports **547,173 evaluated in 2025** ([Fundación EXE](https://fundacionexe.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Analisis-Saber11_2025.pdf)). Coverage of the 2026 results cites 547,063 students at 13,028 school sites, but that figure comes from a search summary ([Diario del Sur](https://www.diariodelsur.com.co/noticias/cambio-ranking-mejores-colegios-colombia-tras-pruebas-saber-11-top-10)). The split has been "casi idéntica" for five years: **about 80% public and 20% private, 82% urban and 18% rural** ([ICFES](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NOTA-POLITICA-Doce-an%CC%83os-del-examen-Saber-11.o-4.pdf)). With no growth in student numbers, revenue can only grow through penetration and price. Each segment pays through a different route.

**Private schools (≈98,000-110,000 students)** have the money and the connectivity. Their tuition is regulated: MEN Resolución 019805 of 2025 caps 2026 increases at **5.26%-9.1%**, and each Secretaría approves the increase before enrolment ([MEN](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/salaprensa/Comunicados/425951:Asi-sera-el-aumento-en-matriculas-y-pensiones-de-colegios-privados-para-2026)). That gives a school three ways to pay. It can absorb the cost in a tuition budget already committed to salaries. It can add an "otro cobro periódico", but those are **"de naturaleza voluntaria"**, must appear in the Manual de Convivencia and cannot cover mandatory course content ([Mineducación](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/micrositios-preescolar-basica-y-media/Educacion-Privada/Tarifas-y-Costos-Educativos/219219:Utiles-uniformes-y-otros-cobros)). Or families can pay the provider directly at a school-negotiated price, which is what Milton Ochoa does for "colegios aliados" at **COP 350,000** ([Milton Ochoa](https://preicfes.miltonochoa.com.co/)). Schools also cannot require a specific provider, and violations carry fines of **50-200 SMLMV** ([Mineducación](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/portal/micrositios-preescolar-basica-y-media/Educacion-Privada/Tarifas-y-Costos-Educativos/219219:Utiles-uniformes-y-otros-cobros)). The upshot: if a school wants every grade-11 student on Aprendo, it pays from its own budget. Otherwise Aprendo is an optional product that families buy directly.

**Public schools (≈390,000-440,000 students)** cannot charge families. They buy through two channels. A rector can contract directly from the school's Fondo de Servicios Educativos below **20 SMMLV, COP 35,018,099 in 2026** (Bogotá figure) ([SED Bogotá Memorando 001-2026](https://www.educacionbogota.edu.co/intrased/sites/default/files/inline-files/MEMORANDO-No001-2026-TABLA-DE-MONTOS-FSE_.pdf)). Municipalities and departments contract through SECOP II. A 2026-11-05 query of the SECOP II dataset found contracts from **COP 50M (Cómbita, Milton Ochoa) to COP 1.74B (Cundinamarca, Milton Ochoa)** ([SECOP CO1.NTC.9914812](https://community.secop.gov.co/Public/Tendering/OpportunityDetail/Index?noticeUID=CO1.NTC.9914812); [CO1.NTC.7872971](https://community.secop.gov.co/Public/Tendering/OpportunityDetail/Index?noticeUID=CO1.NTC.7872971)). Nearly all of them were signed **between mid-January and late July**, before the exam. The largest public buyers make their own programmes. Bogotá's "Saber para Ganar" serves about **40,000 students for free** ([Bogotá.gov.co](https://bogota.gov.co/mi-ciudad/educacion/bogota-fortalece-preparacion-para-prueba-saber-11-con-saber-para-ganar)), and Medellín's SaberEs runs year-round simulacros for **95,642 students** ([Alcaldía de Medellín](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/95-642-estudiantes-de-medellin-inician-los-simulacros-academicos-para-las-pruebas-saber-11/)). Connectivity limits this segment: **63.8% of public and 57.2% of rural school sites have internet**, against 98.8% of private ones. The table headers in the ICFES source conflict on the year ([ICFES, Tabla 1](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NOTA-POLITICA-Doce-an%CC%83os-del-examen-Saber-11.o-4.pdf)).

**Families** already pay for prep, mostly in private, urban, higher-NSE households. Live-virtual courses cost **COP 350,000-770,000** after near-permanent "50% discounts". In-person courses cost **COP 690,000-1.8M** ([Multipruebas](https://multipruebas.co/preicfes-virtual-online/); [Deming](https://www.cursospreuniversitarios.com/preicfes-virtual-bogota/); [Formarte](https://formarte.edu.co/bogota/pre-saber-11/)). Self-paced digital products are much cheaper: PreICFES Fundamental charges **COP 65,000 per course or COP 30,000 a month** ([PreICFES Fundamental](https://preicfesfundamental.com/)), and Código U sells a season pass at **COP 99,900** ([Código U](https://codigo-u.github.io/)). No survey was found on what share of students buy prep. B2C demand cannot be sized from the evidence, only bounded.

| Segment | Students per year | Who pays | Realistic price per student | Ceiling at full penetration (arithmetic, not a forecast) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Private schools | ≈100,000 | School budget, or family voluntarily | COP 45,000-80,000 | ≈COP 4.5-8B |
| Public, via school FSE or municipality | ≈390,000-440,000 | FSE, municipality, department | COP 35,000-50,000 | ≈COP 14-22B |
| Families (B2C) | Unknown share; 10% of the cohort ≈49,000 | Family | COP 89,000-169,000 | ≈COP 4-8B at 10% |

The public column is the biggest pool, but money that is sold through politically timed tenders, in schools where a third of sites lack internet, is the hardest to collect. Benchmarks give a sense of scale: the Cundinamarca contract works out to at least **COP 104,000 per student** if it covered every grade-11 test-taker in the department. That is a lower bound, since it covered only focalised schools ([SECOP CO1.NTC.9914812](https://community.secop.gov.co/Public/Tendering/OpportunityDetail/Index?noticeUID=CO1.NTC.9914812); [Cundinamarca](https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/mas-de-16700-estudiantes-de-cundinamarca-presentan-las-pruebas-saber-11-del-2026)). A digital seat at COP 35,000-50,000 lets a municipality cover two to three times as many students with the same budget.

## The AI tutor is now table stakes; the gap is the teacher workflow

The competitive field has four layers, and each one closes off an easy position. **Traditional chains** (Milton Ochoa, Helmer Pardo, Formarte, Instruimos, Deming, Multipruebas) sell B2C courses and win public contracts. Milton Ochoa is the only national brand seen winning departmental-scale SECOP contracts in 2025-2026 ([SECOP CO1.NTC.9914812](https://community.secop.gov.co/Public/Tendering/OpportunityDetail/Index?noticeUID=CO1.NTC.9914812)). **School assessment providers** hold the private-school budget. iM-PROVE claims **432+ private schools**, including four of the top ten, plus reports by competency and micro-skill, an AI-feedback tutor and a minimum of 25 students per school ([iM-PROVE](https://www.improvecolombia.com/)). Xavia sells **3 virtual simulacros for COP 96,000** per student, also with a 25-student minimum for schools ([Xavia](https://xavia.com.co/servicios/simulacros-saber-11/)). Formarte simulacros sold through a school cost **COP 48,000 for three** in 2024 ([Unilibre circular](https://www.unilibre.edu.co/colegio/pdfs/2024/CIRCULAR%20SIM%C3%9ALACROS%20D%C3%89CIMO-ONCE%202024.pdf)).

The third layer, **AI-first apps launched in 2025-2026**, removes Aprendo's apparent differentiator. Sabero offers a per-question AI tutor that does not reveal the answer, an AI coach, an **IRT score estimate**, spaced repetition and "Licencias para colegios" ([Sabero](https://sabero.co/es)). IAsimulacros charges **COP 26,000 a month or COP 12,000 a week**, has a free tier with four tutor helps a day, and offers a school tier with institution-level metrics and cheating detection ([IAsimulacros](https://www.iasimulacros.com/)). Lumina has a free diagnostic and a "Colegios Pioneros 2026" programme ([Lumina](https://luminaeducation.app/preicfes-con-ia-colombia)). An individual developer has even published a free open-source simulator with IRT and a Socratic tutor ([GitHub](https://github.com/jeveraeng-bit/simulador-icfes)). All these entrants are small (8,000+ registered at IAsimulacros, "hundreds" active at Sabero, all self-reported), so **no digital leader exists yet**. But a "Socratic AI tutor" alone will not win a buyer.

The fourth layer is **free**. ICFES offers an unlimited practice app and booklets ([ICFES guía 2025](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/30-abril-guia-orientacion-saber-11o-2025-2.pdf)). SENA launched a free virtual course in 2025 ([Vanguardia](https://www.vanguardia.com/colombia/2025/08/08/curso-gratis-para-el-icfes-sena-lanza-capacitacion-virtual-aprenda-como-inscribirse/)), and the big cities run their own programmes. These depress willingness to pay most among the public-school families that Aprendo could reach through B2C.

What no one sells cheaply is the combination Aprendo already has in code: a diagnostic, **adaptive practice per subtopic, and a teacher panel with assignments, a week plan and group reports**, priced per seat. iM-PROVE bundles printed books and in-person sessions at premium-school prices. The AI apps are student-first and add school dashboards as an afterthought. That makes the teacher, not the student, the wedge. A coordinator who can assign work to grade 11 on Monday and see weak subtopics by Friday is buying a workflow, and a free ICFES app or a COP 26,000 student subscription cannot replace one. Two more features are common buyer expectations: career and vocational guidance, which appears in Medellín's programme and in Multipruebas, Deming and IAsimulacros ([Medellín](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/95-642-estudiantes-de-medellin-inician-los-simulacros-academicos-para-las-pruebas-saber-11/); [Multipruebas](https://multipruebas.co/preicfes-virtual-online/)), and measurable before/after reports. Aprendo's career-goal methodology covers the first. The diagnostic-to-simulacro arc covers the second.

## International benchmarks favour per-student school licences and exam-length passes

Abroad, the pattern is consistent: **predictable revenue comes from schools, and cheap B2C works only with organic acquisition**. IXL's 2025-26 site licence runs from **US$9.81 per student at 1,000+ seats to US$5.45 at 50,000+**, a 1.8x spread across a 50x volume range ([Henrico County co-op](https://api.henrico.gov/coops/documents/612049578918407119/pdf)). Arco books revenue as enrolled students times an annual ticket per student ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210331005974/en/Arco-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2020-Financial-Results)). Khanmigo for Districts charges separately for the tutor, with a **US$90 list price discounted 33% to US$60 in year one**, which is a ready-made template for a founding-school discount ([BoardOnTrack order](https://app2.boardontrack.com/org/X8qMBm/attachment/publicDownload/293326?s=X8qMBm)). Small minimums are the norm (Kahoot requires three teacher licences) ([Kahoot EDU](https://kahoot.com/schools/edu-school-and-district/)).

For consumers, the closest analogue is Brazil's ENEM. Descomplica sells an **annual pass at 12 × R$32.90 (≈US$6 a month)** and a six-month "Intensivo" for the final stretch ([Descomplica](https://descomplica.com.br/vestibulares/central-de-ajuda/); [Intensivo](https://descomplica.com.br/vestibulares/enem/gabi/)). Exam prep has churn built in: users leave after the exam. A pass that runs until exam day collects revenue up front and matches the user's horizon. Global subscriptions charge Colombia roughly 35-45% of US prices, and **Spotify Individual costs COP 18,500 a month** ([Spotify CO](https://www.spotify.com/co-en/premium/)), a useful ceiling for any monthly plan. Conversion is low, so a free tier needs a large audience. Freemium apps convert at a **median of 2.18%**, against 12.11% for hard paywalls ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/markdown/freemium-to-premium-conversion-techniques.md)). One SaaS sample puts EdTech at 2.6% ([userpilot summary of First Page Sage](https://userpilot.com/blog/freemium-to-premium)). Duolingo needed years to reach **9.0% paid penetration** ([Duolingo Q2 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000165/q2fy25duolingo6-30x25share.htm)).

The failures point in the same direction. BYJU'S grew on hard selling and financing-linked plans and fell from a **US$22B valuation to near zero** ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/from-22-billion-peak-to-debt-traps-and-lawsuits-how-byjus-empire-collapsed-533611-2026-05-28)). Descomplica cut about 292 staff in three rounds, mostly in sales ([Startups.com.br](https://startups.com.br/negocios/demissoes/descomplica-faz-nova-rodada-de-demissoes-e-dispensa-20-do-quadro/)). Chegg lost **~31% of subscribers in a year** once free LLMs commoditised answers ([getcoai](https://getcoai.com/news/edtech-takes-the-f-as-chegg-cuts-22-of-workforce-due-to-ai)). The winner, PhysicsWallah, priced a one-year JEE course at **₹4,500 (≈US$51), 10-20x below incumbents**, and grew through a free YouTube audience to 4.46M paid users ([Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/ipo/physics-wallah-ipo-know-about-the-firm-s-business-model-strengths-and-risks-ahead-of-public-issue/article-184307)). Arco, a pure B2B company, bought Me Salva to reach public-school students its school model missed ([Brazil Journal](https://braziljournal.com/arco-educacao-compra-me-salva-para-entrar-no-b2c)). The lessons for Aprendo: sell the tutor as part of a structured progress system, not as answers. Keep B2C cheap and organic. Do not build a sales machine aimed at families.

## Copyright, IVA and minors' data set the floor before price does

The binding constraint is copyright, not cost. The 2026 Saber 11 booklets state **"Todos los derechos de autor reservados"** and forbid anyone to "vender, distribuir, alquilar, reproducir, **transformar**, promocionar o realizar acción alguna, de la cual se lucre **directa o indirectamente**" from the material. Transformation explicitly includes adaptations, compilations and revisions ([ICFES Cuadernillo Lectura Crítica 2026](https://www.icfes.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/16-feb-cuadernillo-de-preguntas-lectura-critica-saber-11-2026.pdf)). ICFES Concepto 2 of 2015 names commercial PRE-ICFES programmes as its example of illicit use ([Concepto ICFES 2/2015](https://normograma.com/documentospdf/icfes2024/compilacion/docs/concepto_icfes_0000002_2015.htm)). This hits Aprendo directly. Its ingestion pipeline is built around scanned exam PDFs, and the source folder holds ICFES material such as "Matemáticas [Banco de preguntas ICFES].pdf" and "Cuadernillo icfes 2014.pdf" (`/home/war-machine/Documents/repos/aprendo/data/pdfs/`). Because the clause covers indirect profit, a free tier on ICFES items that funnels users to a paid plan or a school licence is also exposed. Paraphrased "tipo ICFES" rewrites count as transformation. No enforcement case against a prep company was found, and the market is full of "tipo ICFES" sellers. But a private school's procurement review or a SECOP evaluator can raise the issue, and a fine or takedown would hit a B2B vendor's reputation hardest. Competitors set the scale of the fix: IAsimulacros claims **1,600+ questions** and Sabero **3,000+** ([IAsimulacros](https://www.iasimulacros.com/); [Sabero](https://sabero.co/es)).

Tax and compliance take a predictable bite. Art. 476 ET excludes from IVA only services of recognised educational establishments, and virtual education "para el desarrollo de contenidos digitales" as regulated by MinTIC ([Gerencie](https://www.gerencie.com/servicios-de-educacion-excluidos-del-iva.html); [MinTIC](https://www.mintic.gov.co/portal/inicio/Sala-de-Prensa/Noticias/63377:Asi-es-la-reglamentacion-de-la-exclusion-de-IVA-en-software-y-servicios-de-educacion-virtual-para-el-desarrollo-de-contenidos-digitales)). DIAN treats web access to general educational content as taxed at the general rate ([DIAN Oficio 016687](https://crconsultorescolombia.com/servicios-excluidos-del-gravamen-dian-oficio-016687.php)). Plan on **19% IVA**: a COP 139,000 IVA-inclusive pass nets about COP 116,800 before gateway fees. Electronic invoicing applies under DIAN Resolución 000165 of 2023 ([DIAN 0165/2023](https://normograma.mintic.gov.co/docs/pdf/resolucion_dian_0165_2023.pdf)). Gateway fees, as reported by 2026 blogs that conflict with each other, run about **2.65% + COP 700 on cards and about 1% on Nequi/QR** through Wompi ([btodigital](https://btodigital.com/pasarelas-pago-colombia-comparativa-guia-negocio/)). The fixed per-transaction fee penalises small monthly tickets and favours passes. Bre-B instant payments, live since October 2025, reach Nequi, Daviplata and bank apps ([Banco de la República](https://d1b4gd4m8561gs.cloudfront.net/sites/default/files/publicaciones/archivos/documento-tecnico-bre-b-febrero-2026.pdf)).

Most grade-11 students are minors, which changes who carries the data burden. Ley 1581 of 2012 allows processing children's data only in their best interest and with authorisation from the legal representative ([SIC concept](https://ambitojuridico.com/sites/default/files/BancoMedios/Archivos/cpto-217036-20.pdf)). SIC Concepto 6089 of 2026 addresses how platforms verify that the parent took part ([GyD Consulting](https://www.gydconsulting.com/medidas-datos-menores-de-edad-plataformas-sic-6089/)). In B2B the school collects authorisation and Aprendo acts as processor (encargado). In B2C Aprendo becomes the controller and must verify the parent, which adds onboarding friction to every family sale. That gives schools a regulatory advantage, not only a commercial one.

The cost that looks largest is the smallest. Aprendo runs `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash` through OpenRouter (`/home/war-machine/Documents/repos/aprendo/packages/convex/src/aiProvider.ts`), listed at roughly **US$0.09-0.15 input and US$0.18-0.30 output per million tokens** ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash)). Under the research notes' assumptions (3,000 input and 400 output tokens per turn, 200 turns a month), a heavy student costs about **US$0.11 a month, or about US$1.1 per school year**. That is roughly COP 4,400 at an unsourced COP 4,000/USD, under 6% of the recommended seat price. Even Claude Haiku 4.5 would cost about US$10-13.5 per heavy student-year ([Token Pricing](https://tokenpricing.pages.dev/pricing)). These figures are estimates and need recalibration with production logs. They show that caps protect against abuse and long HTML demos, not against margin erosion. Prices are set by the cost of writing items, of sales and of support.

## Recommendation: private-school seats, then a cheap pass, then government through partners

### Segment order and why it wins competitively

**First: private schools, B2B seat licence per grade-11 cohort (grade 10 optional).** This segment combines ability to pay, near-universal internet, the cleanest data-protection role and an incumbent (iM-PROVE) priced for premium schools. That leaves the mid-market private school, the bulk of the ≈100,000 students, underserved. The product already models it with `seatLimit` and `licenseEndsAt` (`/home/war-machine/Documents/repos/aprendo/packages/convex/src/schema.ts`). **Second: B2C, an exam-length pass plus a free diagnostic**, run as a low-cost funnel and as the voluntary family-pays route inside private schools, never as a sales-led business. **Third: public sector in two steps.** In 2027, sell single public schools through their FSE under COP 35M, and sell to municipal operators and public universities that already win SECOP tenders (UIS, UTP, Universidad Distrital, PROASEDU appear repeatedly), offering Aprendo as their digital component ([SECOP CO1.NTC.8281696](https://community.secop.gov.co/Public/Tendering/OpportunityDetail/Index?noticeUID=CO1.NTC.8281696); [CO1.NTC.8074196](https://community.secop.gov.co/Public/Tendering/OpportunityDetail/Index?noticeUID=CO1.NTC.8074196)). From 2028, bid directly for small and mid-size municipalities without their own programme, using the 2027 before/after reports as experience certificates. Skip Bogotá and Medellín, which give prep away. "Companies" as buyers are best read as cajas de compensación. Colsubsidio already sells its own course at **COP 417,600-1,183,500 by affiliate category** and Compensar pays a COP 105,000 school subsidy ([Colsubsidio](https://cms.colsubsidio.com/sites/default/files/Documentos/colsubsidio/2025/flipbook-preicfes.pdf); [El Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/colombia/finanzas-y-economia/bono-escolar-confirmado-cajas-de-compensacion-entregaran-hasta-105000-en-enero-de-2026)). That makes them a sponsor channel to test later, not a launch segment.

The steelman for B2G-first is real: it is 80% of students and the contracts are large. It loses on timing and evidence. Contracts favour bidders with experience certificates and outcome reports. The Valledupar contracts drew Contraloría scrutiny when results did not improve ([El Pilón](https://elpilon.com.co/general/denuncian-costosos-simulacros-para-pruebas-saber)). Rural connectivity would also force an offline mode before Aprendo could win department-wide deals. The steelman for B2C-first is cheap reach. It loses to a crowded field at COP 26,000 a month, a median conversion near 2%, the parental-consent burden and free public alternatives.

### Pricing model and price points

Price per student per cycle, with the licence running from purchase to **results day** (late September for calendar A; the 2026 results came out on 25 September) ([El País Cali](https://www.elpais.com.co/colombia/icfes-saber-11-2026-esta-es-la-fecha-de-publicacion-de-resultados-y-como-consultarlos-2758.html)). Do not use calendar-year terms or monthly school billing.

| Public school / operator | FSE, operator, municipality | Colegio Completo; IVA-inclusive quotes | **35,000-50,000** per student by volume | ≥COP 104,000 per student implied in Cundinamarca |

At COP 80,000 + IVA (≈95,200), a single public school can buy about **360 seats under the COP 35M FSE threshold**, enough for nearly any one school's grade 11. Do not publish a results guarantee. No competitor offers one, Lumina states it cannot guarantee a score ([Lumina](https://luminaeducation.app/preicfes-con-ia-colombia)), and outcomes depend on the student. A usage-based refund ("unused seats credited next cycle") lowers risk without promising points. Cap the tutor on every tier (daily turns, output tokens, a stricter cap on HTML demos, and a monthly budget per organisation tied to `seatLimit`), mainly as protection against abuse.

### Prerequisites before charging anyone

**An original item bank** comes first and is non-negotiable. Remove ICFES-derived items from every tier, including the free one, or keep them only in a separate, attributed, non-commercial area that never feeds a paid flow. Target at least 1,500-3,000 original items across the five areas, written against ICFES's public framework of competencies and evidence. Second, **a legal entity registered as an IVA filer, with electronic invoicing**, and IVA-inclusive public quotes. Third, **data-protection paperwork**: an encargado contract template for schools, and verifiable parental authorisation for B2C sign-ups under 18. Fourth, **payments through PSE, Nequi/QR and Bre-B**, and invoicing for schools. Fifth, **a before/after report** generated automatically from diagnostic and final simulacro, because rectors and public buyers increasingly ask for evidence of learning ([Reach Capital](https://www.reachcapital.com/resources/thought-leadership/edtech-en-latinoamerica/)). Sixth, **tutor usage caps** recalibrated from real OpenRouter logs. Seventh, before any department-wide bid, **a low-bandwidth or printable simulacro mode** for rural schools.

## Conclusion

The data turns the obvious framing around. The question is not which customer is largest but which buyer can pay this year, legally, for what Aprendo actually does better. Private schools are the only segment where all three conditions hold. The AI tutor that looked like the moat has turned out to be the cheapest component to build and to run: about a dollar a student a year, copied by at least five rivals and an open-source repo. The scarce assets are an original item bank, a teacher workflow and evidence of impact, so the business should be priced and sequenced around building those three.

The most urgent implication is operational, not commercial. The current bank cannot carry a single paid seat, and the next early-enrolment window for the 2027 cohort closes within weeks. A realistic plan treats school year 2027 as a founding-cohort year: a small set of discounted private schools, an exam-length family pass and partner-led public pilots, all run on original items. The goal of that year is the before/after evidence that unlocks full-price renewals and direct public bids in 2028.
